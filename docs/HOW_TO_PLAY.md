# How to play Saucer Siege

## Objective

Survive six waves of saucers and score as high as you can. Lives: three hull hearts with a 2-second respawn shield after each hit. Your best score is saved on this device. Wave 6's mothership is the final exam.

## The HUD

- **Score** — kills, pods and wave bonuses, multiplied by your combo.
- **Wave** — current of 6. Waves 3 and 6 end with a mothership.
- **Hull** — three hearts. One hit, one heart, 2s of respawn invulnerability.
- **Combo** — kills within 2.5s chain x2 → x8. A hit resets it. Guard it harder than your hull.

## The bestiary (and the bug classes they wear)

| Saucer | Behavior | Counter-play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scout** (RaceCondition) | Drifts in, fires slow rose bolts | Strafe just before the bolt; kill between volleys |
| **Bug** (OffByOne) | Fast zigzag swarm, melee damage | Circles, not lines; they die in one hit |
| **Abductor** (MemoryLeak) | Tractor-beams power pods | Kill it fast — it drops the pod it carried |
| **Mothership** (Deadlock) | Radial bolt rings + aimed fans + minion spawns | Kill escorts first; orbit the pattern, never outrun it |

## Power pods

Glowing hexagons dropped by enemies (~12%, plus every abductor):

- **S — Shield** — absorbs one hit. The bubble is worth more than a heart: take it.
- **T — Triple** — 10 seconds of three-way fire. The combo-builder.
- **E — EMP** — clears every bullet on screen with a shockwave. Save it for the mothership's radial bursts.

## Debriefs

Between waves, the intermission holds a **debrief card** — one concept from the bug-class curriculum. The world pauses while it's up; correct answers pay a wave-scaled bonus and feed your Concepts codex. Wrong answers get the explanation and no penalty. Skip is always one tap.

## Strategy quick-reference

1. Your ship auto-aims — the only real decisions are WHERE you stand and WHAT dies first.
2. Combo beats safety until x5, then survival beats combo. The reset hurts more each tier.
3. Boss waves: enter with a shield or an EMP in the tank. Neither, and you are flying polite.
4. Bugs die to a pass; scouts die to patience. Abductors are always the priority — pods win long fights.
